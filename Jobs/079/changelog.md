🛠️ Update Notes

🪓 You start with a real axe now — it swings, it thuds when it lands, and it sounds like an axe instead of nothing at all.
🔫 Pistols and shotguns are real weapons in your hands, not grey blocks, and each one has its own report — the shotgun racks between shots.
🔥 Your torch is an actual burning brand: a stick, a flame, embers, and light that comes on at dusk.
🎯 The boat's mounted gun finally has a voice — it barks when it fires, clicks dry when it runs out, and you hear it swallow a fresh crate of ammo.
🏮 Camp lights come from real hurricane lanterns sitting on the sandbag line, instead of a glow with nothing making it.
🪵 A new river hazard: tangled log jams float in the channel, and they hit harder than a loose log.
💰 Every trading post has a chest of gold out front, so you can spot the vendor from across the clearing.
👻 Fixed: swinging your weapon no longer throws a mysterious white slab across the screen.
