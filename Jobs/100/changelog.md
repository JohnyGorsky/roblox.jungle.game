# Changelog — Job #100

🏕️ Camps are bigger and no two are laid out the same — you have to actually search them now.

🔦 Loot hides behind huts and cover instead of sitting in the open.

🧱 Camps are fortified with sandbag walls, and which way you break in is up to you.

👀 Raiders no longer appear on top of you — they come in from outside and take up positions.

🌙 Clear a camp and it's yours for a while, but stay too long and they take it back.
