🛠️ Update Notes

🧍 Camp guards stand on the ground. Bandits and wolves were seated three studs into the dirt — buried past the shoulders on a wolf — at every camp on the river.
🎯 You can't snipe a camp for free any more. Shoot anyone in a camp, from any distance, and the whole camp comes for you: they stop caring about their patrol radius and will cross open ground to reach you. Stop shooting and back off, and they'll head home rather than chase you to the boat.
