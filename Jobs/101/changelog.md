# Changelog — Job #101

✨ Loot crates now glow gold as you get near them, so you can tell them apart from the barrels and crates lying around camp.

👀 Get close and the glow shines through walls — no more crates hidden behind a hut you're standing next to.

🤏 You no longer have to stand on top of a crate to loot it.

💰 Gold nuggets glow too — the rarest thing in the game is no longer a small rock among rocks.
