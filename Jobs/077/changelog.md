🛠️ Update Notes

🏕️ Dock camps are real places now — stilt huts, supply tents, sandbag walls, wooden crates and barrels replace the grey boxes you used to raid.
🔥 Every camp has a campfire burning at its centre, with a smoke column you can spot from the river before you reach the landing.
🏛️ The trading post is an actual jungle stilt house with a carved wooden sign, instead of a floating label over a stack of blocks.
🗼 A ranger tower marks each landing that opens a jungle basin, so you can see from the water which stops are worth taking.
⛵ Docks are real timber piers now, not planks — and the boat still ties up exactly where it always did.
✨ Gold nuggets are proper ore instead of a glowing cube.
🌴 Camps sit in a real clearing: dense trees ring the edge, the middle stays open to fight in.
🌙 Camps come alive at night — firelight flickers, lanterns come on at dusk, and fireflies drift through the clearing.
🎧 Camps have sound: fire crackling as you approach, water lapping at the pier, rope creaking while the boat is tied.
🌱 The jungle floor is jungle floor — worn dirt where people walk, grass and mud patches instead of one flat green.
