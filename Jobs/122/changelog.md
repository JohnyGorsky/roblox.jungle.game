# Changelog — Job #122

- 🚀 Tower rocket men now reload far more slowly — the airfield gives you room to fight instead of a shell every few seconds.
- 🎯 They also engage from much closer, so you no longer get shelled from clear across the airfield.
- 🗼 Climb a tower and only the man on it will fight you — the other crews hold their fire instead of dropping a rocket on the platform you are standing on.
