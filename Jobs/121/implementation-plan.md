### 🔴 The constraint that shapes the whole design

`tickGuard` runs off the `CampGuard` **tag** in ExcursionServer's own Heartbeat, and its second line is:

```lua
if not body or not st then guard:Destroy(); return end
```

`st` comes from `guardState`, a **local table**. So a guard spawned from a new EndZone script is
destroyed on its first frame. Manning the end zone therefore means either (a) ExcursionServer builds it,
or (b) ~700 lines of six-times-debugged AI move to a shared module. **(b) was offered and declined**;
(a) is approved as a *minimal hook* — see decisions.

### 🔴 Two intake decisions REVERSED on the reviewer's evidence

**Decision 3 (chasers), 2 → 4.** `tickGuard` does not merely stop a slot-less guard from *approaching* — it
nils its `target`, and the bite is gated on having a target. So a raider without a slot **will not swing at a
player standing next to it.** At a camp's cap of 2 out of ten, eight of the field would have been scenery you
walk straight through, which reads as a broken finale rather than a restrained one. The owner raised it to 4.
Separately, the warlord now carries his **own** camp key so four wolves can never lock him out of the fight —
his 550 HP gates the win, and a passive damage sponge is not a boss. Field ceiling: five attackers of eleven.

**Decision 6 (tower chests), salvage → ammo.** There is no trading post past z = 17600, so salvage in the end
zone pays in a currency the run is about to end with — while the crew that arrives dry, *which is exactly what
requirement 1 is designed to cause*, faces 550 HP with a 15-damage axe. That is 37 connected swings under three
grenadiers behind a gate that will not open. Pistol / Shotgun / Pistol, one per tower.
🔴 **Never M16 or Bazooka**: `pickupLoot` falls back to 12 rounds when a weapon has no `ammoPerCrate`, and
those two deliberately have none — that absence is the only thing capping their rounds against a paid purchase.

## Implementation steps

### 1. `sync/ReplicatedStorage/Enemies/EnemyDefs.luau` — add `BanditBoss`

```
hp 550 (= 55 × 10, the user's number)   speed 20 (Bandit's)
size (6.4, 9.3, 4.8)                   aggroRadius 95
biteRange 16                           biteDamage 10.8   biteCooldown 1.4
category "land"
```

Two things get their own comment block, because both are traps:

- **The hitbox is NOT Bandit's `(5, 4, 11)` × 1.6.** That box is the retired Panther's, kept only because
  Job #058's crew-scaling is tuned against it, and it is 11 studs *deep* — ×1.6 would give an 17.6-stud-deep
  man. `BanditBoss` is a new def with no #058 history (the same argument `RocketMan` makes for itself), so it
  gets an honest box: the R15 human box (4, 5.8, 3) × 1.6.
- **`biteDamage` is Bandit-grade on purpose** (the user's call): 10.8 × `DAMAGE_SCALE` = **5.53**, identical
  to the guards beside him. He is a wall of HP, not a damage spike. `biteRange` 16 rather than 12 because his
  arms are 1.6× longer — a 12 range on a body 1.6× wider would leave him swinging at air.

### 2. `sync/ServerScriptService/Enemies/EnemyAssets.luau` — add `BanditBoss` art

`model = "WesternBandit"`, `scale = 1.6`, `eyes = false`, and the **same** anim/sound tables as `Bandit`
(no new uploads). `EnemyRig` applies scale as `visual:ScaleTo(visual:GetScale() * art.scale)` — relative,
which is what makes 1.6 mean "1.6× how a bandit already looks".

### 3. `sync/ServerScriptService/EndZone/EndGarrison.luau` — NEW, the whole layout lives here

A module, `EndGarrison.build(api)`, called **once**. It owns every position and every number; it borrows
only behaviour. Signature:

```lua
type Api = {
  spawnGuard: (Vector3, Instance, number?, string?, Vector3?, Vector3?) -> Model,
  spawnRocketMan: (BasePart, Model, Instance, number?, Vector3?) -> Model?,
  towerPads: (Model) -> (BasePart?, BasePart?),
  placeOnPad: (string, BasePart, Instance, number?) -> PVInstance?,
  lootPrompt: (PVInstance, string) -> (),
  addFloor: (Instance) -> (),
}
```

**3a. The three grenadiers.** For each `EndBase.Objects.RangerTower`: `towerPads(tower)` → `Defender` pad →
`spawnRocketMan(pad, tower, folder, nil, END_CAMP)`, then
`guard:SetAttribute("FireInterval", 12)`. `towerPads` is called for the side-effect too — it is what makes
the two editor pads intangible and non-queryable (see its own header; without it they are invisible
collidable slabs on the deck).

**3b. The chest on every `InnerPlace` pad.** `placeOnPad(C.model, chestPad, folder, C.scale)` +
`Kind = "Salvage"`, `Amount = 45..70`, `lootPrompt(crate, "Search")` — byte-for-byte the camp recipe, read
from `CampDefs.TOWER.chest` so it restyles with the camps.

**3c. The ten raiders.** Deterministic — `Random.new(END_SEED)` with a fixed seed, because this is
hand-built content and the finale should be the same fight every run (and reproducible to test).

Positions are **computed and validated**, not hardcoded: two rings about (280, 18295) — 4 at r = 30, 6 at
r = 62 — and each nominal angle searches outward through (radius × {1.0, 0.82, 1.18, 0.66, 1.34}) ×
(angle ± up to 0.42 rad) until a slot passes **all** of:

- terrain readable with `Normal.Y > 0.7` and floor y ∈ [14, 26];
- no visible `EndBase` part within 10 studs (`GetPartBoundsInRadius`, `Transparency < 0.99` so the marker
  pads do not count);
- ≥ 16 studs from every already-placed raider, so they read as *spaced*.

**Dry-run result (already executed against the live place, read-only):** 10/10 slots accepted, all on
flat y = 18.00 —

```
(301,18316) (259,18316) (259,18274) (301,18274)          ← inner ring, r=30
(342,18295) (327,18335) (240,18342) (223,18320)
(249,18241) (314,18243)                                  ← outer ring, r=62
```

54–170 studs from the dock, 49–169 from the pad. Type per slot: `rng` picks Wolf or Bandit 50/50 (the
user's "random wolfs or bandits"). If a slot ever fails to resolve, it is **logged and skipped** — a silent
9-of-10 is exactly the kind of quiet truncation GROUND-RULES §8 warns about.

**3d. The boss.** Reserved **first**, at **(345, 18300)** — on the dock→pad line, 46 studs short of the pad,
so he is the last thing between the field and the extraction. Reserving him first means the raider search's
own 16-stud spacing rule pushes the outer-ring slot at (342, 18295) aside rather than stacking on him.

**3e. One camp key for the whole zone.** `END_CAMP = Vector3.new(280, 18, 18295)` passed as every guard's
`campPos`. That single key is what gives the whole end zone **one** shared 2-chaser budget and makes
`alertNear`'s 70-stud spread work sensibly across the field: shooting one raider wakes its neighbours, not
the airfield.

**3f. Parent.** A new `Workspace.EndGarrison` folder. Deliberately **not** inside `EndBase` — Studio Sync is
two-way and `EndBase` is hand-authored content; runtime-spawned rigs have no business in it.

**3g. No respawn — and nothing to do to get that.** The end zone is never registered in ExcursionServer's
`garrisons` list, so the day-shift respawn loop never sees it. Kill the field once and it is yours, the same
rule the hut ambushers and the Rocket Man already follow. The last camp is at z = 17600, **700 studs** from
`END_CAMP` — well outside `GUARD_COUNT_RANGE` (90), so no camp's garrison count is perturbed either.

### 4. `sync/ServerScriptService/Excursion/ExcursionServer.server.luau` — three additions, all opt-in

Approved cross-system edit. **Camp behaviour must be byte-identical**, which is why all three default to
today's values:

1. **`FireInterval` override** in `tickRocketMan`: `local interval = guard:GetAttribute("FireInterval") or R.fireInterval`,
   used in place of `R.fireInterval` at the one comparison. Camps set no attribute → 20 s, unchanged.
2. **`extraFloors`** for the seating raycast — a module-level `{Instance}`, empty for camps, appended to
   `groundAt`'s `Include` filter, plus an `addFloor(inst)` exported through the api.
   **Why this is needed, measured:** `groundAt` filters to `Terrain` only. The extraction pad sits *on the
   runway*, whose top is **21.11** against terrain **18.00** — so a guard chasing a player near the pad
   would walk at 18.00 and stand **3.1 studs sunk into the concrete**, buried to above the knee. The
   boss's leash (55 from x = 345) reaches x = 400, and the runway starts at x ≈ 365, so this is not
   hypothetical, it is his whole post. `EndGarrison` registers **only the three `RunWay` models** — not
   `EndBase` wholesale, which would make a tower platform at y = 61 or the plane's fuselage at y = 34.77
   answer as "floor". Both are above `groundAt`'s existing `CLEAR_Y + BASIN_RISE` = 23 clamp and would be
   rejected anyway; the runway's 21.11 sits just under it, which is the only reason this works at all.
3. **The build hook** — one `task.spawn` near the tail that calls `EndGarrison.build` **once** when either:
   - the boat crosses `z = END_ZONE_Z_START − 900` (17100) — the crew is at the last camp, so all 14 rigs
     exist before anyone can see the field, nothing pops in; **or**
   - any player is inside the EndBase footprint — because `AdminServer`'s `tpEndBase` jumps a tester there
     **without moving the boat**, and without this clause the button lands you in an empty end zone and the
     feature reads as broken.

### 5. `sync/ServerScriptService/Enemies/EnemyServer.server.luau` — the sea gauntlet

**5a. The surge.** `FINAL_SURGE_FROM = RiverData.END_ZONE_Z_START - 800` (z 17200) and
`FINAL_SURGE_MUL = 3` (the user's ×3). Applied in two places: the `seaCap()` cap **before** `crewScale`
(so a solo player keeps their 0.55 discount) and the sea spawn interval (÷3).

Numbers derived from the existing formulas rather than asserted:

| | today at the end | in the surge |
|---|---|---|
| cap, 6-crew day | 10 | **30** |
| cap, 6-crew night | 13 | **39** |
| cap, solo day | 5 | **16** |
| sea spawn interval, night | 1.33 s | **0.44 s** |

The interval cut is not optional dressing: the boat covers 800 studs in ~32 s, and at 1.33 s per spawn the
cap could never be reached before the mouth — the ×3 would have been a number with no observable effect.
Crocs, piranha and hippos are **single MeshParts** with no per-frame raycast on the sea path, so 39 is
cheap; the visual density is the thing to judge, not the frame time.

**5b. Spawn-point water guard, end zone only.** Past z ≈ 18200 the *generated*-river formula stops
describing the *sculpted* basin: `lockFactor` pins `centerlineX` to 0 and `widthAt` to 300 for all z ≥
18000, but the real channel has curved east to x ∈ [50..150] by z 18300. Spawning on the formula there puts
a crocodile on a hillside at y = 43. So for `z ≥ END_ZONE_Z_START − 200` only:

- clamp the spawn z to ≤ `END_ZONE_Z_START + 150`;
- and require the point to actually be terrain water (down-ray with `IgnoreWater = false` sitting
  meaningfully above the same ray with `IgnoreWater = true` — the same test used to probe the basin above).

**Gated to the end region on purpose.** Applying the water test everywhere would suppress legitimate
mid-river spawns whenever the chunk 320 studs ahead has not been generated yet — turning a safety check
into a density regression. Upstream of 17800 the channel is water by construction.

**5c. Stop the riverbank land spawner at the mouth** — `hull.Position.Z < RiverData.END_ZONE_Z_START` added
to its existing gate, so boars stop appearing once the airfield fight begins (decision 7).

### 6. `sync/ServerScriptService/EndZone/EscapeServer.server.luau` — the boss gates the win (decision 10)

🔴 **FAIL-OPEN, and that is the whole design of this gate.** A bug in `EndGarrison` must never make the run
unwinnable, and `RunServer`'s only win arm is `Workspace.EscapeReached` — there is no other way out of a
run except dying. So the gate is expressed as a **positive liveness flag**, not as a kill requirement:

- `EndGarrison` sets `Workspace:SetAttribute("EndBossAlive", true)` **only when the boss rig actually
  spawns**, and clears it the moment his `HP` attribute hits 0 (watched via `GetAttributeChangedSignal`,
  with an `AncestryChanged`/`Destroying` backstop in case `EnemyRig.destroy` removes him without a final
  HP write).
- `EscapeServer` refuses to `extract()` while `EndBossAlive == true`, and says so on the sign.
- If the attribute was never set — no `EndBase`, the boss slot failed to resolve, `EndGarrison` errored,
  the module is missing — the gate simply is not there and the pad works exactly as it does today.

The occupancy loop's copy changes to name the blocker (`"Kill the warlord first"`) rather than silently
refusing, and the force-launch prompt is disabled while he lives — a prompt you can hold that does
nothing is worse than no prompt.

**Second-run reset.** On `RunStarted` going true, `EndGarrison` drops its built flag, destroys the old
`Workspace.EndGarrison` folder and clears `EndBossAlive`, then rebuilds on the next trigger. Without this an
admin-forced second run in the same server walks into a field of corpses and — worse — a **stale
`EndBossAlive = true` from a boss who no longer exists, which would be an unwinnable run.** `BunkerServer`
already resets off this exact attribute for the same reason.
