# Changelog — Job #075

🎨 The whole in-game interface has been rebuilt to match the lobby — same wood-and-canvas panels, same warm cream lettering, no more grey placeholder boxes.

🚤 The HUD now changes with what you're doing: gathering at the wreck, riding the river, or ashore raiding a camp — each shows only what matters right then.

🗺️ A new river map across the top tracks how far you've come, names the zone you're in, and marks the next fuel stop — and warns you in red when your tank won't reach it.

⚙️ Man a station and the boat finally tells you what you're earning the crew: sharper steering, thriftier fuel, faster turret, a patched hull.

⚠️ Leave the boat at a dock and you can now see it from inland — its hull, how far back it is, and a warning the moment something starts biting.

📱 Purpose-built touch controls for the helm: steering under your left thumb, throttle under your right.
