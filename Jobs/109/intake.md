## Requirements / goal

Four items from playtest feedback on Job #108.
1) DEFECT from #108: the two wooden blocks beside each hut door are the EntryStep treads I built. Every Bahay Kubo ALREADY has its own staircase at its doorway (confirmed by measurement and by the user), so the treads are redundant AND they clutter/obstruct the entrance. Remove them, and orient each hut by its STAIR bearing rather than by the widest-clear-arc bearing (they differ on BahayKubo5).
2) No feedback when you gain anything. Looting a crate, searching a hut stash and killing an enemy all pay out silently, so the player has to guess. Add ONE generic reward message shown whenever a player receives something - salvage from any source, a picked-up resource, a weapon or ammo. The distance drip must stay silent or it will spam.
3) The village reads as bare houses on a lawn. Scatter rocks, logs, undergrowth and a few trees around the huts, plus some pickable things outside, so the place feels lived in.
4) Two of the village huts, chosen at random, each get ONE bandit who sits inside and waits. Two additional bandits per landing site, not per hut.
