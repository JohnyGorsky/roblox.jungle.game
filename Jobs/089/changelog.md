🛠️ Update Notes

💾 Your progress is now handled by a battle-tested save system. Gold, boat modules, skills and paint move between the lobby and the river the moment you teleport, instead of waiting on a timer that could hand you a stale copy of your own save.
🧾 Robux purchases are safer. A purchase is only marked complete once it is actually written to your save — so a server hiccup at the wrong moment can no longer take Gold you paid for. If your save can't be reached at all, the game now refuses the purchase rather than swallowing it.
