# Changelog — Job #102

🏕️ Your first landing is gentler — one raider in the first camp and a pair in the second, so you can learn the fight instead of being swarmed on the beach.

⏱️ Raiders now come back. Clear a camp and reinforcements walk in from the treeline every 2–3 minutes — before, a cleared camp could stay empty for the best part of ten.

🚶 You can see them coming. Reinforcements arrive from outside the fence and walk to their posts instead of appearing on top of you.

🩹 Every enemy in the game hits 20% softer — on the river and ashore.

🧭 New crew are told what to do. Step off the boat for the first time and the game tells you to raid the camps inland for fuel, metal and ammo.
