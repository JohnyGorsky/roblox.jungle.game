# Changelog — Job #103

❤️ Getting revived now brings you back at **full health** — no more standing up on half a bar.

🛡️ You are **invulnerable for 10 seconds** after a revive, so you get a chance to run instead of being cut down the moment you stand up.

⏱️ A countdown shows exactly how long you have left, and you glow gold while it lasts — your crew can see you're protected too.

💰 Both revives get it: the Robux self-revive and a teammate's bandage.
